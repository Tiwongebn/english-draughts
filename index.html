    How to play

    Black moves first. Men move diagonally forward; kings move and capture in all four directions. If a capture is available you must take it, and you must keep jumping while further jumps exist. A man that reaches the far row is crowned and its turn ends. The game is drawn after 40 moves each without a capture or a man move. Move highlighting (pieces that can move, their destination squares, captures and the "you must capture" notice) is always on in Easy and optional in Medium and Hard, where it starts switched off.
